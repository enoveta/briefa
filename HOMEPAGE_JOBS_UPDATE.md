# Briefa homepage jobs update

- The homepage Jobs section now uses a compact navy-and-cyan intro panel beside four selected job cards.
- Job cards no longer use large photos; they use an organization-style monogram mark, employer name, job title, summary, location/deadline details, and View Details link.
- The homepage preview is intentionally limited to four selected roles and links to the full Jobs page.
- The full Jobs page is limited to five job listings per page total (one featured listing plus four compact listings), with Previous/Next pagination when more jobs are available. Other non-News listing pages keep their existing six-item pagination.
- Existing navy (#00225B) and cyan (#00D7FD) brand colors are retained.

Note: the current project data does not consistently include official organization logo image URLs. Where those are unavailable, cards use a clean initials-based organization mark rather than inventing a logo. Add an `organizationLogo` URL to story data to display a verified logo in future.
